# Issue #76 — Phase B Trader Summary

The repaired Wyckoff Regime Radar is more useful as an **exposure governor** than as a symmetric Buy/Sell signal generator.

## FX

- **Accumulation:** reduce long conviction; weak short caution only.
- **Markup:** normal long exposure is allowed, but the regime alone does not justify adding risk.
- **Distribution:** strongest long-supporting state in this sample; this is a classifier-behavior fact even though the classical label sounds bearish.
- **Markdown:** the short bias is concentrated at fresh entry and should decay quickly; generic mature occupancy is much weaker.

## Rates (yield-level sign; duration is opposite)

- **Accumulation:** neutral duration early; persistent/mature Accumulation shifts toward reduced duration / mild pay-rates bias.
- **Markup:** short duration / pay rates, strongest after the state survives roughly 1–9 bars.
- **Distribution:** long duration / receive rates; relatively clean because yield-up tail and future volatility are lower than baseline.
- **Markdown:** long duration / receive rates, strongest early; mature Markdown still favors lower yields but deserves less size because volatility rises.

## Research meaning

The next step is to freeze a small exposure policy from these distributions (for example Strong / Normal / Reduced / Flat), not to optimize entries, stops, targets or holding periods. Only after that policy is frozen should its profitability be tested OOS.
