- Rebuild wealth index from all 1960+ monthly equity returns; SMA10 compares index at end-t with the average of end-t through t-9; MOM12 compares the previous 12 months of compounded equity and cash returns using sums of log(1+r). Both are **applied in month t+1**, never to month t's payoff.
- Reconstruct 2bp per absolute target-weight change (10bp sensitivity), omit initial deployment costs consistently; compute geometric CAGR, sample annualized volatility, monthly peak-to-trough MaxDD, excess-to-cash Sharpe-like, average equity weight, switches and four preregistered era CAGRs.

The corresponding full-period and era calculations reproduce the archived result to the displayed precision. ## Literature-grounded volatility design — no results

- **Faber (2007 / later updates):** end-month 10-month SMA long/cash. The current single-equity index implementation is an adaptation, not the entire multiasset GTAA paper.
- **Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012):** cross-asset **futures** 12-month time-series momentum with long/short and volatility scaling. The current equity-only relative-to-cash long/cash MOM12 is **not** an original-paper replication.
- **Moreira & Muir (2017):** use *prior month's daily realized variance* of a factor's returns to scale next month's factor **excess** return, typically `f_vm[t+1] = (c / RV[t]) × f[t+1]`; a full-sample constant `c` matches unconditional volatility to the unmanaged factor. This paper-style scaling can introduce leverage and **full-sample ex-post normalization**, so cannot be described as an ex-ante unlevered equity timing rule. Authors' slides: https://amoreira2.github.io/alan-moreira.github.io/SlidesVol.pdf ; article: https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12513 .

**Required separation before third-strategy results:**
1. *Paper-fidelity replication*: frozen daily market excess factor, realized variance from last month's daily market-factor returns, paper's normalization; explicitly label ex-post volatility match and unconstrained factor/leverage exposure. Compare risk-adjusted performance to its **own** unmanaged factor, not directly claim investable superiority to 0–100% SMA/MOM.
2. *Deployable apples-to-apples comparison* (requires a **new design prereg before any volatility P&L**): monthly one-month-lag decisions, common 1967-03..2026-08 payoff panel, **0..100% equity/no leverage**, same cash and cost/rebalancing assumptions, no sample-informed thresholds or ex-post scaling, and controls matched by observed average exposure as *descriptive hindsight*. The choice of pre-sample-only scaling/calibration and risk constraint must be frozen before seeing outcome, otherwise **BLOCK** the three-way comparison.
3. Only after (1) and (2) are reliable should GPI/IPI/FCPI be tested for **incremental** value under the same protocol.

**Stop condition:** Official daily source/reconciliation blocked → Moreira–Muir and three-way risk-control ranking remain **unverified**. Do not replace official French data with an unlabelled mirror or monthly standard deviation, claim strategy victory, merge PR #195, or modify Pine.
